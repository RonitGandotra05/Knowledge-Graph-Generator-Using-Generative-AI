From research paper to knowledge graph.

A knowledge graph represents concepts as nodes and their relationships as connections. Evidence Atlas helps you explore those connections across research documents, with the original quotations close at hand.

Turn PDFs into an AI knowledge graph

  1. Upload your papers. Add up to 10 documents in the research workspace. PDFs are parsed in your browser. Scanned pages use automatic English OCR; unreadable pages and excluded files are reported.
  2. Add an optional focus. Describe your research question or enter a few keywords. The AI uses this context to help identify relevant concepts, alongside concepts discovered in the paper.
  3. Connect your provider. Choose OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Groq or a compatible API, then enter your model and key. Review the estimated tokens, provider cost and duration before starting.
  4. Watch the graph grow. Concepts and supported relationships appear as document sections are analyzed. Processing is paced to the selected provider's limits.

The tool works across research fields. A computer science paper might connect a model to a training method; a materials paper might connect a composition to a measured property. It does not require a biological vocabulary.

Follow relationships back to the source

Click a node or relationship to inspect the source document, paragraph and quoted evidence. PDF references use the PDF page index, which can differ from a page number printed inside the paper.

A quotation matching the extracted text does not establish that an AI interpretation is correct. Read the surrounding paragraph, check the relationship's direction and distinguish a reported association from a causal claim. OCR can also introduce errors.

When comparing multiple papers, use the source references to see which document supports each connection. Treat the graph as a way to navigate the literature, rather than proof that every important finding has been captured.

Edit your graph and keep an offline copy

Rename or recolor nodes, edit relationships, delete unwanted concepts, and undo or redo changes. Original evidence remains attached and edits are marked. Switch between circles and cards, drag nodes, use optional physics, search, zoom or open fullscreen.

Export interactive HTML for an offline viewer, JSON to preserve graph data, or PNG and SVG images. Choose the light or dark theme you want before exporting. Explore the illustrative sample graph to try the controls without an API key.

Common questions

Is Evidence Atlas free?

The application charges $0. Your chosen AI provider may charge for API usage. Groq's published rate limits describe its free tier; availability and account quotas can change. A ChatGPT subscription does not include OpenAI API usage.

Are my API key and documents stored on this website?

Your API key stays in the tab's memory and is erased on refresh. Uploaded files are parsed locally; selected excerpts go directly to your AI provider when analysis starts. Parsed text, graphs and draft checkpoints are saved in this browser's local history, without your key or original files. Your provider has its own retention policy.

Can I leave the homepage while a graph is building?

Switching between the homepage and workspace keeps analysis running. New graph saves the current project to History first. Refresh clears the key and interrupts the active requests; reopen the saved draft and re-enter your key to continue. Keep exports of important work.

Will it find every relationship in a paper?

No AI system can promise complete or error-free extraction. Coverage reports show which passages and pages were processed, and source evidence helps you review the result. Use your own research judgment and the original documents to assess missing or uncertain findings.
