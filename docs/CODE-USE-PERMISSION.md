## Why the repository uses permission-required terms

The requested policy was that people need permission to reuse the original code. MIT instead gives advance permission for broad use, modification and distribution, subject to its notice requirements. Consequently, the repository includes a custom permission-required notice and does not claim an MIT or open-source license. See the [official MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit) and [GitHub’s repository licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository).
