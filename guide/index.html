Turn PDFs into an AI knowledge graph

  1. Upload. Add up to 10 papers in the workspace. PDF text and scanned pages are read locally; unreadable pages are reported.
  2. Focus. Optionally add a research question or keywords. Leave blank to explore the whole paper.
  3. Connect. Choose your AI provider, model and API key. Review the estimated time, tokens and cost.
  4. Build. Click Build my graph. Concepts and supported relationships appear as the papers are analyzed.

Works across research fields. Your provider may charge for API use; the app charges $0.

Follow relationships back to the source

  1. Inspect. Click a node or connection. Its evidence opens inside the graph, with the paper, page, paragraph and quote.
  2. Go to this line. Open the original PDF in a compact popup in the same tab. Matching lines are highlighted. Use + / − to zoom, Fit width to reset, and drag or scroll to read around them.
  3. Reattach if needed. History and offline HTML do not store the original PDF. Choose the same source file when prompted.
  4. Verify. Read the surrounding text and check the relationship's direction. AI and OCR can be wrong; a matching quote does not prove the interpretation.

PDF page numbers refer to the file's page order, which may differ from printed page numbers. Missing or unmatched text is reported rather than highlighted.

Edit your graph and keep an offline copy

  • Explore. Search, zoom, drag nodes or open fullscreen. Choose circles, cards, ovals or diamonds. Drag the evidence inspector by its header; arrow keys move it too.
  • Customize. Open Customize to resize the whole graph, change node and connection text sizes, or shorten connections. Try Compact for a smaller graph. Hide connection labels when you want a cleaner overview.
  • Edit. Add concepts, rename and recolor them, and keep personal notes. Inside Edit concept, open a connection to change its endpoints, description or delete it. + Connection joins any two concepts; your additions are marked as yours, without claiming paper evidence.
  • Select. Use Select multiple, click concepts or drag a selection box, then Delete selected. Shift-click also selects a concept. Connected relationships are removed together. Undo and redo recover graph edits and deletions; original evidence stays attached.
  • Save. Drafts save automatically in this browser. New graph keeps your previous work in History.
  • Export. Choose HTML for an interactive offline graph, JSON for graph data, or PNG / SVG for an image. Set your preferred theme first.

Common questions

Is Evidence Atlas free?

The application charges $0. Your chosen AI provider may charge for API usage. Groq's published rate limits describe its free tier; availability and account quotas can change. A ChatGPT subscription does not include OpenAI API usage.

Are my API key and documents stored on this website?

Your API key stays in the tab's memory and is erased on refresh. Uploaded files are parsed locally; selected excerpts go directly to your AI provider when analysis starts. Parsed text, graphs and draft checkpoints are saved in this browser's local history, without your key or original files. Your provider has its own retention policy.

Can I leave the homepage while a graph is building?

Switching between the homepage and workspace keeps analysis running. New graph saves the current project to History first. Refresh clears the key and interrupts the active requests; reopen the saved draft and re-enter your key to continue. Keep exports of important work.

Will it find every relationship in a paper?

No AI system can promise complete or error-free extraction. Coverage reports show which passages and pages were processed, and source evidence helps you review the result. Use your own research judgment and the original documents to assess missing or uncertain findings.
